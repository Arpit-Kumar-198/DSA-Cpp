INCREASING ORDER OF TIME COMPLEXITY (FASTEST TO SLOWEST)

+------+----------------+----------------+
| No.  | Time Complexity| Name           |
+------+----------------+----------------+
| 1    | O(1)           | Constant       |
| 2    | O(log n)       | Logarithmic    |
| 3    | O(sqrt(n))     | Square Root    |
| 4    | O(n)           | Linear         |
| 5    | O(n log n)     | Linearithmic   |
| 6    | O(n^2)         | Quadratic      |
| 7    | O(n^3)         | Cubic          |
| 8    | O(n^4)         | Polynomial     |
| 9    | O(2^n)         | Exponential    |
| 10   | O(3^n)         | Exponential    |
| 11   | O(n!)          | Factorial      |
+------+----------------+----------------+

FASTEST: O(1)
SLOWEST: O(n!)
